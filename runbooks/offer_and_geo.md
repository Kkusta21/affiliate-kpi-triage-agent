# Offer and geo issues

## Offer cap reached
When an offer reaches its daily cap the advertiser rejects further conversions. Tell the affiliate so they can shift traffic, and ask the advertiser for a cap increase if the campaign is profitable.

## Offer paused
A paused offer converts nothing. Confirm the reason and the restart time with the advertiser and tell the affiliate to stop traffic.

## Geo mismatch and disallowed country traffic
Traffic from a country the offer does not accept will not convert. Send the affiliate the list of allowed countries and ask them to fix targeting. Consider a geo redirect to a matching offer.
