# Traffic quality

## Bot traffic from a sub source
A sub source with a high bot score and a large share of clicks drags conversion rate down and risks advertiser chargebacks. The affiliate manager reviews the sub source, holds payout for it and decides whether to block it. Blocking is a manual decision and is never automated.

## Fraud review
Collect click logs for the flagged sub source, compare IP and user agent diversity, and ask the affiliate for the placement. Document the decision in the incident.
