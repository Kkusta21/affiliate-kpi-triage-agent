# Landing page down

## Landing page outage
When the landing page returns 5xx or times out while clicks keep arriving, every click is wasted spend for the affiliate. Page tech on-call immediately. Ask the affiliate to pause traffic or switch to the backup landing page. Confirm recovery with a fresh probe before closing the incident.

## Slow landing page
Latency above 10 seconds counts as down for mobile traffic. Check the CDN status and the last deploy. Treat as an outage if it lasts more than 15 minutes.
